# Metric dictionary

| Metric | Numerator | Denominator | Date logic / exclusions | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contact compliance | Active scoped students with a complete proof-backed contact | Active scoped students requiring contact | Latest contact within baseline 7 days; incomplete contacts excluded | Operations |
| Graduation rate | Scoped students meeting the group's accepted paid USD gig rule | Active scoped students | Current acceptance; no manual graduation; original non-USD values excluded until approved conversion | Project Operations |
| Overdue actions | Open tasks with due timestamp before now | Not a percentage | Completed actions excluded | Task owner |
| Evidence backlog | Coach/L1/Quality/L3 pending submissions | Not a percentage | Current stage; order oldest stage first | Review owner |
| Review SLA breaches | Pending items older than 24h Coach/L1 or 48h Quality | Not a percentage | Calendar hours, no business-hour calendar configured | Coach Ops / Quality Lead |
| Critical recommendation | Students meeting computed critical triggers | Not a percentage | Attendance <50%, two milestones behind, five failed attempts in 14 days, or second rejection | Supervisor |

Baseline policy: Round 5 v1. Synthetic fixtures begin with no valid contacts or accepted evidence. Forecasts, proof-audit score and group graduation trajectory are not invented; full metric policy and historical inputs remain required.
